Privacy by design

Private family trust is not inventory.

(re:) separates family life from commercial participation. Private messages, family relationships, care information, youth activity, personal financial inputs, and private archive content are protected surfaces—not ad targeting material.

Family-owned context

Family records are designed to remain isolated to the family space, with authorization boundaries that get stronger as production infrastructure comes online.

Explicit relationships

Connected families, sponsors, providers, and partners do not automatically gain access to private family data or authority.

Intentional sharing

Information only moves into outside services through explicit consent and purpose-built workflows—not because the platform can technically see it.

FAQ

The short version.

Is (re:) a social network?

No. Social features exist because families communicate, but the product is centered on continuity: relationships, gatherings, shared responsibility, legacy, care, learning, opportunity, and long-term family context.

Who owns the family data?

The product direction treats family-owned records as tenant-isolated family data. Outside organizations, sponsors, partners, and providers do not receive automatic access.

Is the prototype fully production-ready?

The current experience is a focused pre-backend product. Many workflows function locally; production authentication, cross-device sync, payments, cloud storage, delivery, and server-enforced authorization are the next infrastructure boundary.

Can I see the working app?

Yes. Open the current interactive prototype at /app.html.
